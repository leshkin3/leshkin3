Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer same-day appliance repair in Vancouver, WA? ▼
Yes! If you call early in the day, we can often dispatch a technician the same day. We know a broken fridge or washer is an urgent situation, so we make every effort to get there fast.
How much does a service call cost? ▼
We charge a service call fee to come out and diagnose the problem. If you proceed with the repair, that fee is applied toward the total cost. You'll always know the price before any work begins.
What areas do you serve around Vancouver, WA? ▼
We serve Vancouver and the surrounding Clark County area, including Camas, Washougal, Battle Ground, Ridgefield, La Center, and nearby communities. See our full service area below.
Do you work on high-end brands like Sub-Zero, Viking, or Miele? ▼
Absolutely. We have experience with premium and luxury appliance brands and use OEM (original equipment manufacturer) parts to ensure the repair meets the manufacturer's standards.
What's included in the 90-day warranty? ▼
The warranty covers both parts and labor for 90 days after the repair. If the same issue comes back within that period, we'll fix it at no additional charge.
Can you repair commercial appliances? ▼
Yes — in addition to residential appliances, we also service commercial equipment. Contact us to discuss your specific needs and we'll let you know if it's a good fit.
